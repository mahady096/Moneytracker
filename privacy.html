Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

Money Tracker is a personal finance tracker. This page explains what data the app handles.

Data we store

Your email address (or Google account identifier) for sign-in, and the financial information you enter yourself: institutions, accounts, balances, records, schedules, budgets and categories. The app does not connect to your bank and does not read your messages, contacts or location.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored in your private area of Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Access is restricted by security rules so that only you can read or change it.

How it is used

Only to show your own data back to you inside the app. We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or show ads.

Your control

You can download a backup at any time (Reports) and permanently delete your account and all your data (Settings, "Delete my account and all data").

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change.
